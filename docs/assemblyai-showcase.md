## If they ask a follow-up

The one-sentence version of why AssemblyAI is load-bearing rather than decorative:

> Remove AssemblyAI and Beacon does not lose its voice interface — it loses the
> right to hold the credential at all. The words are what a change to production is
> justified by afterwards, and every property of that justification comes from the
> API: what unlocked the change, how clearly it was heard, which channel it came
> from, and a recording to play back.

Numbers, if wanted: median 3.45 s to first audio over 15 real calls at telephone
quality ([`docs/assets/benchmark.md`](assets/benchmark.md), generated by `make bench`
from reports written during the calls), and a live phone approval re-scored at 95 %
with `fix` as its weakest word, found at 40.1 s on the caller's channel.
